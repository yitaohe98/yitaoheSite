After watching someone work through a live bug-bounty session, I realized the process felt tedious and did not seem to create much of a compounding asset. It appeared closer to trading time and energy for uncertain rewards, and I suspect parts of the work may become increasingly automated by AI. For now, I’ve decided not to continue in this direction.

Instead, I’m shifting my focus toward iOS apps, using Android as a reference. Existing app stores offer a more natural way to observe demand and reach users, so I’ve started using MobileAction—a tool similar to Semrush for researching app keywords, search volume, and competition. The current goal is to choose a small idea and ship something within the next week.
